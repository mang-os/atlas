# Flight interface direction
Source: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design
Purpose: select a sample departure, temporarily hold capacity, confirm before its deadline.
Palette: airline blue #1754CC, navy #132B50, ink #182C49, white #FFFFFF, neutral #F5F6F8, line #D9DFE8.
Type: Segoe UI/Arial, tabular departure times and countdown. A single family supports the reservation task.
Layout: horizontal airline masthead, compact search, aligned departure rows, persistent booking summary. Real route codes and times carry the visual identity.
Review: removed the dashboard sidebar, promotional hero, decorative plane orbit, rounded badge collection and aspirational copy. Flight controls and booking state now occupy the first screen.
Principles: blue is reserved for actions; time, route, fare and availability have explicit hierarchy. No gradients, animated decoration, invented amenities or payment form. Fictional-flight disclosure stays visible.
